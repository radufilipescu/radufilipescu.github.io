# Paladin sword-and-shield provenance

Source: the user-supplied `Pro Sword and Shield Pack.zip`, containing `Paladin WProp J Nordstrom.fbx` and 51 animation FBXs. The model and animations are Mixamo assets, subject to [Adobe's Mixamo terms](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html), **not CC0 or open source**. The source model filename credits J Nordstrom. This conversion does not grant permission to redistribute raw assets.

The conversion preserves authored geometry, skinned sword/shield/helmet and bone rotations. It normalizes overall size and orientation and makes horizontal hip travel in-place while retaining vertical motion. The 51 original clips remain available. CombatRoll and DrinkPotion are locally generated actions; StrafeRightMirrored and RunStrafeRightMirrored reflect source leftward footwork/core motion while preserving arm and equipment handedness. Their generator scripts and hashes are recorded in the manifest. These four clips are not original actions from the source pack. PotionGrip is a metre-scaled right-hand attachment for the runtime flask; the sword is hidden while the flask is presented. Embedded 2048-pixel diffuse and normal maps are preserved. The legacy specular map remains in the original archive; runtime PBR uses metallic 0.35 and roughness 0.5 as an approximation. Sword collision samples are evaluated at 960 Hz from the final GLB's own animation curves.

Run `python scripts/build_fighter_assets.py` after project setup with `--art-tools`. The source ZIP is read from `assets/fighters/` and extracted into ignored `.tools/art-source/mixamo/`. The converter makes no downloads. The manifest pins the archive, source files, output GLB, collision samples, clip indices and durations.
